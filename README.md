🌐 ConnectCare
🏥 AI-Powered Healthcare Companion

Connecting patients and doctors for smarter, safer recovery.

🚀 Overview

ConnectCare is an intelligent healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and doctors through real-time monitoring, AI assistance, and seamless communication.

It enables patients to track their health, perform daily check-ins, and stay connected with healthcare providers — all from the comfort of their home.

✨ Features

🔹 Patient Dashboard

View health metrics and reports

Daily health check-ins

AI-based suggestions

🔹 Doctor Dashboard

Monitor patient status in real-time

Access patient reports

Manage multiple patients efficiently

🔹 AI Assistant 🤖

Smart symptom analysis

Personalized health recommendations

Previous query tracking

🔹 Authentication System

Secure login/signup

Role-based access (Patient / Doctor)

🔹 Health Monitoring

Continuous tracking of patient conditions

Alerts for abnormal health data

🧠 Problem It Solves

During recovery, small symptoms often go unnoticed but can lead to serious complications.
ConnectCare helps detect early warning signs and ensures timely medical attention — potentially saving lives.
